Deploy: Deadman Switch

    Before you deploy for real, run one full test with a 3-minute check-in and a 9-minute period of inactivity (the smallest values allowed): deploy, reply to arm, reply to a check-in, then stay silent and watch the warning and the trigger arrive. Twelve minutes now is worth more than any amount of reading.

    Welcome to Deploy: Deadman Switch service. Here’s how it works. You will receive a Check-In at regular intervals. When you receive the email, reply to it with the short code it contains — that reply, from any phone or computer, is what resets the timer. Emails contain no links, and there is no other way to check in: if replying ever stops working, this page will say so, and the switch should be aborted until mail is fixed. If the Period of Inactivity elapses without a check-in, the Deadman Switch will trigger and your emails will be sent. All your data is encrypted until the final emails are sent. You can send up to 20 emails.

    Deploy also asks each recipient to confirm their address by replying with a code — their reply is the only real proof the address still reaches them. It is on by default and we recommend leaving it that way; with it off, we cannot validate the communication channel. If you uncheck this box, your beneficiary will remain in the dark (and potentially unreachable) until the deadman switch is triggered.
