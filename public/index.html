Deploy: Deadman Switch

    Welcome to Deploy: Deadman Switch service. Here’s how it works. You will receive a Check-In at regular Intervals. When you receive the email, open it and click the link to log your activity — clicking that link is the only thing that resets the timer. If the Period of Inactivity elapses without a check-in, the Deadman Switch will activate and your emails will be sent. All your data is encrypted until the final emails are sent. You can send up to 20 emails.

    Deploy also asks each recipient to confirm their address with a one-click link — their click is the only real proof the address still reaches them. It is on by default and we recommend leaving it that way; with it off, an address that has quietly died is never discovered. You can turn it off for any individual recipient when you edit their message, if that person must learn nothing until it arrives.
