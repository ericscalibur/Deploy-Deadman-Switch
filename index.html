The Deadman Switch

If you stop checking in, your designated emails are automatically sent. Set it and forget it — until you can't.

How it works
Three simple steps. No accounts, no servers.
1

Set your check-in interval

Choose how often you want to receive a check-in email — daily, weekly, monthly.

2

Set your inactivity period

Define how long without a check-in before the deadman switch activates.

3

Add your recipients

Add up to 20 email addresses. If the switch activates, they all receive your message.

Features
Simple by design
🔒

No accounts required

Self-hosted and runs locally. Your data never touches a third-party server.

⏰

Live countdowns

Real-time timers show exactly when your next check-in is due and when the switch would activate.

📧

Up to 20 recipients

Add as many trusted contacts as you need. Everyone gets notified when the switch fires.

🛡️

Open source

Fully auditable code. No black boxes. You know exactly what runs when the switch activates.
